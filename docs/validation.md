# Validation and interpretation

The workbook contains 303 records. Performance estimates use 5-fold nested stratified cross-validation. For every outer fold, algorithm selection uses only the outer training partition and shuffled 4-fold inner CV. The winning algorithm is calibrated within that training partition, then scored on the untouched outer test partition. The outer test partition never participates in model selection. Preprocessing and model fitting occur inside folds. Candidate algorithms are Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost. Logistic Regression and Random Forest calculate class weights from fold training labels; XGBoost is unweighted. Selection uses mean inner ROC-AUC, with mean inner F1 as a tie-breaker. Separately, 4-fold CV over all development records chooses the final deployment algorithm after nested evaluation; this selection does not produce the reported nested metrics.

The final deployment pipelines use sigmoid calibration. Reported metrics and plots use nested outer-fold predictions from the calibrated estimator selected separately in each outer training partition. Summary tables show fold means and standard deviations plus pooled outer out-of-fold scores. Brier score and calibration curves accompany accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, confusion matrices, and ROC curves. Outer-fold risk display thresholds are derived only from their corresponding outer training partition. Final deployment display bands are derived separately from the full development fit and do not determine evaluation performance. These are internal results on one small dataset, not external validation or evidence of clinical readiness.

`LOW`, `MODERATE`, and `HIGH` are prototype display bands, not clinical risk categories or cutoffs. The system predicts overall CAD for `Cath` and vessel stenosis for LAD, LCX, and RCA; vessel-level probabilities do not identify anatomical lesion location, extent, or presence in an individual.

SHAP output explains model probability contributions for the four targets and is presented in raw feature space. It is descriptive of model behavior, not causal evidence. Individual artery probabilities do not identify the anatomical location, extent, or presence of a lesion in a particular patient.
